UGC Gazette Notification Approval

Gazette Notification No. F.5-1/2013 (CPP-II) • July 5, 2014

The Bachelor of Naturopathy and Yogic Sciences (BNYS) program is duly approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC) as per Gazette Notification No. F.5-1/2013 (CPP-II), published on July 5, 2014. The University is empowered to confer a BNYS degree in accordance with its statutory powers and the norms prescribed by the UGC.

Ministry of AYUSH Exemption: NEET Is Not Required

As per the Ministry of AYUSH Order No. R 14011/06/2018 EP II, dated 25 February 2019, the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET) is not required for admission into the Bachelor of Naturopathy & Yogic Sciences (BNYS) program. Admissions are granted directly based on academic eligibility and merit.

Eligibility Criteria & Academic Duration

Admission Guidelines

Eligibility Criteria for BNYS

Students who have completed 10+2 in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB) or Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) from any recognized board/institute are eligible for Bachelor of Naturopathy and Yogic Sciences admission.

  • 10+2 with PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) OR
  • 10+2 with PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics)
  • Recognized Central / State Education Board or Equivalent
  • Merit-based direct admission (NEET exemption active)

Program Duration

The program shall be spread over five and half academic years (5.5 Years), spread over four and half years (4.5 Years) comprising actual teaching for a minimum of 180 days in each year, and Internship Training for one Year (1.0 Year) in the fifth year.

  • Total Duration: 5.5 Academic Years
  • Academic Instruction: 4.5 Years (9 Semesters / Annual Terms)
  • Minimum Instruction Days: 180 Teaching Days per Academic Year
  • Compulsory Rotatory Internship: 1 Full Year (12 Months)
Phase 1: 4.5 Academic Years

Academic & Clinical Foundations

Comprising actual teaching for a minimum of 180 days in each year. Intensive classroom education, biochemical & physiological laboratory training, diagnostic methodologies, yogic therapies, pathology, naturopathic modalities, and supervised hospital clinical observations.

Phase 2: 1.0 Year (12 Months)

Compulsory Rotatory Internship

Full-time clinical internship wherein candidates conduct actual clinical practice of Naturopathic Modalities, diagnostic management, and holistic patient care under the direct mentorship of experienced doctors and faculty members.

Internship & Dissertation / Project Work

Practical Training

Dissertation / Project Work

An elective course designed to acquire special/advanced knowledge, such as supplement study/support study to a project work, and candidate studies such a course on his/her own with an advisory support by a teacher/faculty member is called dissertation/project work. A Project/Dissertation work may be given in lieu of a discipline specific elective paper.

Compulsory Rotatory Clinical Internship (12 Months)

Internship duration: 12 months.
Internship is a phase of training wherein a graduate student is expected to conduct actual practice of Naturopathic Modalities and acquire skills under the supervision of a competent faculty member so that he/she may become capable of working independently. A project work is to be carried out by the student individually during this training phase.

Program Outcomes (POs)

Graduate Competencies

After completion of the BNYS program, the graduate student must:

PO 1
Clinical Diagnostics & Health Management: Be able to expertly diagnose and manage common diseases and health problems of individuals as well as the community, work with the health team as a fully qualified doctor at primary, secondary and tertiary levels, with his/her clinical experience and skills.
PO 2
Humane & Holistic Patient Orientation: Develop a humane attitude towards one’s clients and understand their economic, environmental, social, psychological and cultural factors that influence health.
PO 3
Therapeutic & Preventative Medicine: Be proficient in promotive, preventive, curative and rehabilitative medicine and therapy for common health issues.
PO 4
Healthcare Administration & Leadership: Possess management skills in human resources, materials and resources management in health care delivery.
PO 5
Professional Ethics & Compassion: Develop integrity, responsibility, reliability, dependability and compassion, which are characteristics required for successful professional life.
PO 6
Mastery of Therapeutic Modalities: Be adept in different therapeutic modalities and their administration in clinical and hospital environments.
