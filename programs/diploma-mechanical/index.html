Program Overview

The Diploma in Mechanical Engineering (Polytechnic) at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya equips students with robust technical and foundational skills in design, analysis, fabrication, and maintenance of mechanical machinery and power systems. Rooted in both theoretical principles and comprehensive shop-floor training, the program shapes students into industry-ready technicians and junior engineers.

Students work with contemporary engineering tools including AutoCAD, SolidWorks, CNC machining, welding simulators, and material testing rigs. Lateral entry is available directly into the 2nd year (3rd semester) for eligible 10+2 Science/Vocational or 2-year ITI pass-outs. Graduates also enjoy a seamless direct lateral entry pathway into the 2nd year of B.Tech Mechanical Engineering.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Foundation in Applied Sciences & Engineering Graphics

Core Subjects:

  • Applied Mathematics-I — Algebra, trigonometry, calculus basics for engineering
  • Applied Physics-I — Mechanics, properties of matter, heat & acoustics
  • Applied Chemistry — Fuel chemistry, water technology, lubricants & polymers
  • Engineering Graphics & Drawing — Orthographic projections, isometric views, lettering
  • Communication Skills in English — Technical vocabulary, report writing, phonetics

Practical Labs: Physics Lab, Chemistry Lab, Workshop Practice (Carpentry & Fitting).

Semester 2: Engineering Mechanics & Workshop Technology

Core Subjects:

  • Applied Mathematics-II — Integral calculus, differential equations, vectors
  • Engineering Mechanics — Statics, dynamics, friction, trusses, center of gravity
  • Basic Electrical & Electronics — AC/DC circuits, transformers, semiconductor diodes
  • Workshop Technology — Foundry, smithy, sheet metal, welding processes
  • Environmental Science & Green Tech — Ecology, pollution control, industrial safety

Practical Labs: Engineering Mechanics Lab, Basic Electrical Lab, Machine Shop Practice.

Semester 3: Mechanics of Materials & Thermodynamics (Lateral Entry Point)

Core Subjects:

  • Strength of Materials — Stress-strain, bending moment, shear force diagrams, torsion
  • Applied Thermodynamics — Laws of thermodynamics, air cycles, steam boilers, nozzles
  • Machine Drawing with CAD — Sectional views, fasteners, 2D drafting on AutoCAD
  • Manufacturing Science-I — Lathe, shaper, drilling operations, cutting tool geometry
  • Materials Science & Metallurgy — Crystal structures, heat treatment, alloy steels

Practical Labs: Strength of Materials Lab, Thermal Lab, CAD Lab-I.

Semester 4: Fluid Power & Manufacturing Systems

Core Subjects:

  • Fluid Mechanics & Machinery — Bernoulli's theorem, pumps, Pelton/Francis turbines
  • Theory of Machines — Kinematic chains, gears, cams, governors, flywheel
  • Manufacturing Science-II — Milling, grinding, broaching, non-traditional machining
  • Electrical Drives & Control — Motors, starters, PLC basics in mechanical automation
  • Metrology & Quality Control — Linear & angular measurements, comparators, SPC

Practical Labs: Fluid Mechanics Lab, Manufacturing Practice-II, Metrology Lab.

Semester 5: Machine Design, Power Engineering & Automation

Core Subjects:

  • Design of Machine Elements — Shafts, couplings, keys, bolted & welded joints
  • Internal Combustion Engines & Refrigeration — 2/4 stroke engines, vapour compression cycles
  • CNC Machines & CAM — G-codes, M-codes, CNC turning and milling programming
  • Industrial Engineering & Operation Research — Plant layout, work study, inventory control
  • Elective: Automobile Engineering / Mechatronics

Practical Labs: CNC & CAM Lab, IC Engines & RAC Lab, Minor Project Initiation.

Semester 6: Industrial Management, Plant Maintenance & Capstone Project

Core Subjects:

  • Power Plant Engineering — Thermal, hydro, nuclear, and renewable energy plants
  • Plant Maintenance & Safety Engineering — Breakdown/predictive maintenance, OSHA norms
  • Entrepreneurship & Industrial Management — Business plans, project management, ethics
  • Elective: Robotics / Additive Manufacturing (3D Printing)

Capstone & Internship: Major Project Fabrication, 4-Week Industrial Internship & Defense.

Admission & Lateral Entry Options

Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya offers multiple entry and progression pathways for mechanical diploma students:

  • Regular Entry (3 Years / 6 Semesters): Passed 10th Standard with Mathematics and Science from any recognized state/central board.
  • Lateral Entry to 2nd Year (2 Years / 4 Semesters): Passed 10+2 Examination with Physics, Mathematics or Chemistry/Vocational Subjects, OR 2-year ITI Certificate in Mechanical/Fitter/Turner/Machinist trade.
  • Progression to B.Tech: Diploma graduates are eligible for direct Lateral Entry into 2nd Year B.Tech Mechanical Engineering at DSVV or other AICTE-approved institutions nationwide.
