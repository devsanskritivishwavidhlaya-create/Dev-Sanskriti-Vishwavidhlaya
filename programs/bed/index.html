Program Overview

The Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.) program at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya is designed to prepare competent, compassionate, and culturally rooted educators. The program integrates modern pedagogical techniques with DSVV's philosophy of value-based holistic education.

Students gain hands-on teaching practice through school internships, micro-teaching sessions, and supervised classroom teaching. The curriculum covers educational psychology, classroom management, inclusive education, and technology-aided teaching methods.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Foundations of Education

Core Subjects:

  • Philosophy of Education — Indian & Western educational thoughts (Gandhi, Vivekananda, Tagore, Dewey, Rousseau)
  • Educational Psychology — Learning theories, motivation, intelligence, individual differences
  • Child Development & Pedagogy — Cognitive, social, emotional development; age-appropriate teaching
  • Communication Skills & Language — English/Hindi proficiency, classroom communication

Practical / Lab:

  • Micro-Teaching Sessions, Psychology Lab, Language Lab

Semester 2: Curriculum & Assessment

Core Subjects:

  • Curriculum Studies — NCERT/NCF framework, curriculum design, syllabus construction
  • Educational Assessment & Evaluation — CCE, formative/summative assessment, item analysis
  • Methods of Teaching — Subject-specific pedagogy (Science, Mathematics, Social Studies, Languages)
  • Gender Studies in Education — Gender sensitization, inclusive classroom practices

Practical / Lab:

  • Lesson Planning Workshop, Peer Teaching, ICT Lab

Semester 3: Advanced Pedagogy & Practice

Core Subjects:

  • Classroom Management — Behavior management, positive reinforcement, conflict resolution
  • ICT in Education — Smart boards, e-learning tools, educational software, digital content creation
  • Inclusive Education — Special needs education, learning disabilities, differentiated instruction
  • School Management & Leadership — Educational administration, school organization, leadership skills

School Internship I:

  • 4-week school observation and practice teaching in affiliated schools

Semester 4: Teaching Practice & Research

Core Subjects:

  • Educational Research Methodology — Action research, case study, questionnaire design
  • Environmental Education — Sustainability, eco-consciousness, global citizenship
  • Guidance & Counseling — Student counseling, career guidance, mental health support

Internship & Project:

  • School Internship II — 8-week supervised full-time teaching practice
  • Minor Research Project — Action research in classroom settings with report submission
  • Comprehensive Viva Voce — Oral examination covering entire B.Ed. curriculum
