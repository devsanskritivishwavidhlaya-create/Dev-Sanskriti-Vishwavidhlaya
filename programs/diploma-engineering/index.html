Programs Overview

The Polytechnic Engineering Diploma programs at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya are crafted to nurture job-ready technocrats and field engineers. Blending rigorous theoretical understanding with intensive laboratory sessions, machine shop training, CAD modeling, and industrial field internships, these programs provide a dynamic launchpad into India's core engineering sectors.

Both regular 3-year entry (after 10th Standard) and direct 2nd-year lateral entry (after 10+2 Science/Vocational or 2-year ITI) are available. After graduation, students can either immediately step into prestigious public/private sector technician jobs or advance via direct lateral entry into the 2nd year of B.Tech.

Diploma in Mechanical Engineering

Focuses on thermodynamics, manufacturing technology, CAD/CAM, fluid mechanics, SOM, and automobile mechanics. Prepares junior engineers for PSUs, railways, and automotive OEMs.

Diploma in Civil Engineering

Covers surveying, concrete technology, structural mechanics, water resources, highways, estimation & costing. Prepares site supervisors and draughtsmen for infrastructure firms and PWD.

Diploma in Electrical Engineering

Emphasizes electrical machines, power transmission, sub-station switchgear, PLC automation, and renewable energy. Prepares junior engineers for state power boards and industrial plants.

Eligibility & Lateral Entry Pathway

  • Regular Admission (3 Years): 10th standard pass from a recognized board with Science & Mathematics.
  • Lateral Entry (2 Years / Direct 3rd Semester): 10+2 with Science (PCM/Vocational) or 2-year ITI in the relevant trade.
  • B.Tech Lateral Entry: All diploma graduates are eligible for direct 2nd Year admission into B.Tech Engineering across universities.
