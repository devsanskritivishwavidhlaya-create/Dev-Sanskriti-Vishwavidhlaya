Program Overview

The B.A. programs at DSVV combine liberal arts education with the university's core philosophy of value-based learning. Students receive a well-rounded education integrating humanities, social sciences, and cultural studies with practical skills development.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1 & 2: Foundation

Language papers (English/Hindi), Foundation courses in Social Sciences, Environmental Studies, Indian Heritage, and Computer Basics.

Semester 3 & 4: Core Discipline

Core subject papers, elective courses, research methodology basics, and practical assignments.

Semester 5 & 6: Advanced & Project

Advanced specialization papers, project work, internship component, and comprehensive examination.
