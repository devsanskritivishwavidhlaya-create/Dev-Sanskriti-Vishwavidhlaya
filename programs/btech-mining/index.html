Program Overview

The Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Mining Engineering at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya is crafted to meet the critical workforce needs of India's mineral-rich heartland, particularly Chhattisgarh and surrounding coal, iron ore, bauxite, and limestone mining hubs. The program provides comprehensive training in the safe, economical, and sustainable extraction of mineral resources.

Students receive rigorous instruction in surface open-cast mining, underground coal and metal mining, geological exploration, precision mine surveying with GPS/Total Station, rock mechanics, advanced drilling & controlled blasting, ventilation network analysis, heavy earth-moving machinery (HEMM), and DGMS statutory mining regulations.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Engineering Fundamentals

Core Subjects:

  • Engineering Ethics & Mining Jurisprudence — Ethical resource stewardship, DGMS overview, professional responsibility
  • Communication & Technical Writing — Technical report generation, mining logbooks, presentation techniques
  • Engineering Mathematics - I — Differential calculus, matrix algebra, differential equations
  • Computer Programming using C — Structured programming, data structures basics, computing logic
  • Basic Electrical & Electronics Engineering — Circuits, mine electrical distribution, motors and drives
  • Introduction to Mining Engineering & Mineral Resources — Mineral distribution of India, mining terminology, life cycle of a mine
  • Engineering Graphics & CAD — 2D/3D drafting, projection geometry, computer-aided design

Semester 2: Core Engineering & Geological Foundations

Core Subjects:

  • Engineering Mathematics - II — Multivariable calculus, series solutions, probability and statistical distributions
  • Engineering Physics & Geology Basics — Wave mechanics, crystallography, optical mineralogy, petrology
  • Engineering Chemistry & Mineral Analysis — Geochemistry, assaying, coal proximate analysis, fuels and combustion
  • Engineering Mechanics & Fluid Machinery — Statics, kinematics, fluid mechanics, pumping principles
  • Basic Workshop Technology — Machine shop, foundry, welding, metal forming
  • Environmental Science & Sustainability — Ecology, mine environmental baseline studies, conservation
  • Computing & Geology Lab — Mineral identification, rock thin-section microscopy, C programming

Semester 3: Mining Technology & Exploration

Core Subjects:

  • Mining Geology & Mineralogy — Structural geology, faults, folds, ore genesis, economic mineral deposits
  • Elements of Mining Technology — Shaft sinking, tunneling, drift development, roadway drivage
  • Mechanics of Solids & Strata Basics — Stress-strain relations, bending moments, shear stresses, rock core logging
  • Mine Surveying - I — Triangulation, traversing, leveling, EDM, compass surveying
  • Mineral Exploration & Reserve Estimation — Geophysical prospecting, geochemical surveys, borehole drilling, reserve categorization
  • Fluid Mechanics & Mine Hydraulics — Mine dewatering, slurry transport, pipe friction losses
  • Mine Surveying Lab - I — Theodolite traversing, leveling exercises, surface contour mapping

Semester 4: Extraction Technologies & Geomechanics

Core Subjects:

  • Surface Mining Technology — Opencast bench design, stripping ratio, box cuts, haul road engineering
  • Underground Coal Mining — Bord and pillar method, longwall mining, continuous miners, depillaring strategies
  • Drilling & Blasting Engineering — Commercial explosives, blasting caps, blast design, vibration monitoring, flyrock control
  • Mine Surveying - II — Underground correlation surveys, gyro-theodolite, stope surveying, GPS & Total Station
  • Rock Mechanics & Ground Control — Rock mass classification (RMR, Q-system), intact rock testing, failure criteria
  • Heavy Earth Moving Machinery (HEMM) — Shovels, draglines, dumpers, bucket wheel excavators, hydraulic maintenance
  • Rock Mechanics & Blasting Lab — Compressive and tensile strength of rocks, seismograph vibration testing

Semester 5: Advanced Mining & Ventilation

Core Subjects:

  • Underground Metal Mining — Stoping methods (sublevel, cut and fill, block caving), stope preparation, backfilling
  • Mine Ventilation & Environmental Engineering - I — Mine atmosphere, noxious gases, natural and mechanical ventilation, fans
  • Mineral Dressing & Processing Technology — Crushing, grinding, sizing, froth flotation, gravity separation, magnetic separation
  • Mine Hazards & Safety Engineering — Coal dust explosions, firedamp, spontaneous combustion, inundation, DGMS guidelines
  • Slope Stability in Surface Mines — Planar, wedge, circular failure, factor of safety, rock reinforcement and anchors
  • Mining Machinery & Materials Handling — Mine winders, skips, cage systems, conveyors, locomotive haulage
  • Mine Ventilation & Air Quality Lab — Anemometers, psychrometers, gas detectors, ventilation network computer simulation

Semester 6: Numerical Modeling, Legislation & Economics

Core Subjects:

  • Mine Ventilation & Environmental Engineering - II — Heat and humidity, air refrigeration, ventilation network planning, auxiliary ventilation
  • Advanced Rock Mechanics & Numerical Modeling — FLAC, UDEC, Phase2 boundary element modeling, support design
  • Mine Management Legislation & General Safety (DGMS Norms) — Mines Act 1952, Coal Mines Regulations (CMR), Metalliferous Mines Regulations (MMR)
  • Mine Economics Feasibility & Valuation — Discounted cash flow (DCF), net present value (NPV), mine cost estimation, mineral royalties
  • Geostatistics & Mine Planning Software — Semivariograms, kriging, block modeling, Datamine / Surpac / AutoCAD mining packages
  • Mine Automation & Robotics — Autonomous haulage, tele-remote drilling, drone surveying, smart mine sensors
  • Mineral Processing & Testing Lab — Ball mill grinding, sieve analysis, flotation cell concentration

Semester 7: Planning, Reclamation & Deep Mining

Core Subjects:

  • Mine Planning & Design (Surface & Underground) — Ultimate pit limit design, production scheduling, stope layout optimization
  • Mine Environmental Impact Assessment & Reclamation — Overburden dump management, acid mine drainage, afforestation, closure plans
  • Mine Rescue Disaster Recovery & Emergency Operations — Rescue apparatus, breathing bags, refuge chambers, emergency command
  • Deep Seam & Coal Bed Methane (CBM) Mining — High-stress mining, rock bursts, gas drainage, underground coal gasification (UCG)
  • Minor Project & Industrial Training Report — Vocational training in mechanized opencast / underground mine, report and seminar
  • Mine Design & Computer Simulation Lab — Mine planning software practicals, pit design, blast optimization modeling

Semester 8: Capstone Project & Field Vocational Residency

Core Components:

  • Major Capstone Project — Comprehensive engineering project in mine design, geomechanics, ventilation planning, or mineral processing
  • Mine Vocational Training & Industrial Internship (12 Weeks) — Mandatory on-site training in Coal India (SECL/WCL), NMDC, SAIL, or top private mining firms
  • Comprehensive Viva Voce — Final defense and oral examination covering entire 4-year curriculum conducted by DGMS / industry panels
