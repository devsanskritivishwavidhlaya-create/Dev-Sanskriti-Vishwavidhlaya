Program Overview

The Post Graduation Diploma in Business Management (P.G.D.B.M.) at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya is crafted for ambitious graduates seeking an accelerated executive management qualification. The program blends contemporary corporate business frameworks with value-based ethical leadership and practical managerial problem-solving.

Covering marketing strategy, financial modeling, human resource administration, supply chain logistics, and business research, PGDBM prepares professionals to make data-driven, strategic decisions in dynamic Indian and global markets.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Core Functional Management & Analytics

Core Subjects:

  • Management Principles & Organizational Behavior — Leadership, group dynamics, motivation, organizational design
  • Managerial Economics & Business Environment — Demand forecasting, cost structures, macroeconomic policies
  • Accounting for Managers — Financial reporting, ratio analysis, cost accounting, budgeting controls
  • Marketing Management — Marketing mix, customer segmentation, digital marketing channels, consumer behavior
  • Quantitative Techniques & Business Statistics — Probability, hypothesis testing, regression analysis

Practical / Lab:

  • Excel for Business Modeling, Harvard-style business case study analysis, and group presentations

Semester 2: Strategic Leadership, Integration & Capstone Project

Core Subjects:

  • Strategic Management & Corporate Policy — Industry competitive analysis (Porter's model), diversification, growth strategies
  • Financial Management — Capital budgeting, working capital management, cost of capital, dividend decisions
  • Human Resource Management & Labor Laws — Talent acquisition, performance appraisal, industrial relations
  • Operations & Supply Chain Management — Inventory control, quality management (TQM/Six Sigma), logistics
  • Entrepreneurship & Venture Development — Business plan drafting, startup funding, incubation strategies

Practical / Lab:

  • PGDBM Capstone Research Dissertation, 8-Week Corporate Internship Report & Final Defense Viva
