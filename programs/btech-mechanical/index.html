Program Overview

The Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Mechanical Engineering at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya is designed to produce skilled mechanical engineers capable of designing, developing, and maintaining mechanical systems across industries. The program integrates theoretical knowledge with hands-on practical experience through workshops, CAD/CAM labs, and industry internships.

Students gain expertise in core areas including thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, manufacturing processes, machine design, robotics, and automotive engineering. The curriculum is regularly updated to align with industry standards and emerging technologies like Industry 4.0, additive manufacturing, and electric vehicles.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Foundation

Core Subjects:

  • Engineering Mathematics - I — Calculus, linear algebra, differential equations
  • Engineering Physics — Mechanics, optics, thermodynamics fundamentals
  • Engineering Chemistry — Materials science, corrosion, water treatment
  • Basic Electrical Engineering — Circuit theory, AC/DC machines

Practical / Lab:

  • Physics Lab, Chemistry Lab, Electrical Lab, Workshop Practice

Semester 2: Core Engineering

Core Subjects:

  • Engineering Mathematics - II — Complex analysis, transforms, numerical methods
  • Engineering Graphics & CAD — Technical drawing, AutoCAD, SolidWorks
  • Programming in C / Python — Computational thinking, algorithm design
  • Workshop Technology — Foundry, welding, machining fundamentals

Practical / Lab:

  • CAD Lab, Programming Lab, Workshop Lab

Semester 3: Mechanical Core I

Core Subjects:

  • Engineering Mechanics — Statics, dynamics, free body diagrams, trusses
  • Strength of Materials — Stress, strain, bending, torsion, deflection
  • Thermodynamics — Laws of thermodynamics, entropy, Carnot cycle, gas power cycles
  • Manufacturing Technology - I — Casting, forging, rolling, extrusion

Practical / Lab:

  • Strength of Materials Lab, Thermodynamics Lab, Manufacturing Lab

Semester 4: Mechanical Core II

Core Subjects:

  • Fluid Mechanics & Machinery — Fluid properties, Bernoulli's equation, pumps, turbines
  • Heat & Mass Transfer — Conduction, convection, radiation, heat exchangers
  • Manufacturing Technology - II — CNC programming, welding, sheet metal
  • Machine Drawing & Solid Modeling — Assembly drawings, 3D modeling (SolidWorks, CATIA)

Practical / Lab:

  • Fluid Mechanics Lab, Heat Transfer Lab, CNC Lab

Semester 5: Advanced Mechanical

Core Subjects:

  • Theory of Machines — Mechanisms, cams, gears, vibrations, balancing
  • Machine Design - I — Design of shafts, keys, couplings, bearings, brakes
  • Automobile Engineering — Engine components, transmission, chassis, EV basics
  • Industrial Engineering & Management — Production planning, ERP, lean manufacturing

Practical / Lab:

  • Theory of Machines Lab, Machine Design Lab, Automobile Lab

Semester 6: Electives & Specialization

Core Subjects:

  • Robotics & Automation — Robot kinematics, PLC programming, industrial automation
  • Machine Design - II — Design of gears, springs, clutches, flywheels
  • Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) — CFD simulation, ANSYS Fluent

Elective I (Choose One):

  • Renewable Energy Systems
  • Composite Materials
  • Quality Control & Reliability
  • Operation Research

Practical / Lab:

  • Robotics Lab, CFD Lab, Elective Lab

Semester 7: Industry & Research

Core Subjects:

  • Finite Element Methods (FEM) — FEA simulation, ANSYS Workbench
  • Power Plant Engineering — Thermal, hydro, nuclear power plant design & operations
  • Engineering Ethics & Professional Practice

Elective II (Choose One):

  • Electric Vehicles & Hybrid Systems
  • Additive Manufacturing (3D Printing)
  • Tribology & Lubrication
  • Advanced Machining Processes

Practical / Lab:

  • FEM Lab, Power Plant Lab, Industry Visit & Report

Semester 8: Capstone Project

Core Components:

  • Major Project — Independent or team-based engineering design / research project
  • Comprehensive Viva Voce — Oral examination covering entire B.Tech curriculum

Internship (Mandatory):

  • Minimum 8-week industry internship / industrial training with report submission
