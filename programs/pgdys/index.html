Program Overview

The Post Graduate Diploma in Yogic Science (P.G.D.Y.S.) at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya integrates ancient Vedic wisdom with modern physiological and scientific understanding. This premier program equips graduates to understand human mind-body dynamics, master classical yogic texts, and implement personalized yoga therapy for preventive and curative health.

Conducted in an authentic yogic and spiritual atmosphere, students undergo rigorous practical training in Shatkarmas, Asanas, Pranayama, Bandhas, Mudras, and Meditation, preparing them for esteemed careers as certified yoga professionals and lifestyle consultants.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Foundations of Yogic Science & Physiology

Core Subjects:

  • Foundations of Yoga & Classical Scriptures — Origin, historical evolution, Patanjali Yoga Sutras, Bhagavad Gita principles
  • Anatomy & Physiology for Yoga — Musculoskeletal, respiratory, cardiovascular, nervous, and endocrine systems
  • Hatha Yoga Texts — Hatha Yoga Pradipika, Gheranda Samhita, Shiva Samhita tenets
  • Yogic Lifestyle & Stress Management — Dinacharya, Ritucharya, Yama-Niyama psychology

Practical / Lab:

  • Shatkarmas (Jala Neti, Sutra Neti, Vamana Dhauti, Kapalbhati), Sukshma Vyayama, Surya Namaskar, Classical Asanas

Semester 2: Yoga Therapy, Human Consciousness & Applied Healing

Core Subjects:

  • Yoga Therapy for Common & Chronic Disorders — Diabetes, hypertension, spinal issues, anxiety, asthma, digestive ailments
  • Human Consciousness & Yogic Psychology — Pancha Kosha theory, Kundalini, Chakras, Nada Yoga, Vipassana & Preksha Dhyana
  • Naturopathy Principles & Dietetics — Five elements theory, hydrotherapy, mud therapy, Satvik nutrition
  • Teaching Methodology of Yoga — Lesson planning, class alignment, ethical codes, safety considerations

Practical / Lab:

  • Advanced Asanas, Pranayama protocols, Therapeutic Yoga demonstration, 4-Week Clinical Internship & Comprehensive Viva
