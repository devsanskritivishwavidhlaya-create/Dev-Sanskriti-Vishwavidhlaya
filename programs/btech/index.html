Program Overview

The B.Tech programs at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya combine rigorous engineering education with the university's philosophy of value-based learning. Students gain hands-on experience through state-of-the-art laboratories, workshops, and industry internships.

The curriculum is designed to produce competent engineers who are not only technically proficient but also socially responsible, equipped with ethical values and practical problem-solving skills.

Year-by-Year Curriculum

Year 1: Engineering Fundamentals

Engineering Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Basic Electrical Engineering, Engineering Graphics, Programming Fundamentals, Communication Skills, and Environmental Science.

Year 2: Core Engineering

Data Structures, Circuit Theory, Thermodynamics, Strength of Materials, Analog & Digital Electronics, Control Systems, and core branch-specific subjects.

Year 3: Advanced Specialization

Advanced core subjects, Elective papers, Power Systems / Software Engineering / Structural Analysis, Laboratory sessions, and Mini Project.

Year 4: Capstone & Industry

Major Project, Industrial Training / Internship, Elective specialization, Seminar, and Comprehensive Viva. Industry-ready skill development.
