Program Overview

The Diploma in Civil Engineering (Polytechnic) at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya prepares aspiring engineers to plan, construct, supervise, and manage infrastructure projects including roads, bridges, water supply systems, and modern residential and commercial complexes.

Blending foundational mathematical and physical sciences with extensive practical fieldwork, students master total station surveying, GPS mapping, soil mechanics testing, concrete quality inspection, and architectural software suites such as AutoCAD and Revit. Eligible 10+2 (Science/Vocational) or 2-year ITI (Draughtsman Civil/Surveyor) candidates can secure direct admission to the 2nd year via lateral entry.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Foundation in Applied Sciences & Basic Drafting

Core Subjects:

  • Applied Mathematics-I — Trigonometry, coordinate geometry, matrices, differentiation
  • Applied Physics-I — Measurements, elasticity, surface tension, heat transfer
  • Applied Chemistry — Water treatment, corrosion, cement and binding chemistry
  • Engineering Graphics & Drawing — Scale drawing, projections, isometric elevations
  • Communication Skills in English — Grammar, presentations, professional correspondence

Practical Labs: Physics Lab, Chemistry Lab, Workshop Practice (Carpentry, Sheet Metal, Masonry).

Semester 2: Engineering Mechanics & Construction Materials

Core Subjects:

  • Applied Mathematics-II — Integration, differential equations, numerical methods
  • Applied Mechanics — Force systems, friction, center of gravity, moment of inertia
  • Building Materials — Bricks, timber, stones, lime, paints, ferrous and non-ferrous metals
  • Basic Electrical & Mechanical Engineering — Basic circuits, pumps, site equipment
  • Environmental Science & Disaster Management — Earthquake hazards, ecology, sustainability

Practical Labs: Applied Mechanics Lab, Building Materials Testing Lab.

Semester 3: Surveying-I & Mechanics of Structures (Lateral Entry Point)

Core Subjects:

  • Surveying-I — Chain surveying, compass, plane table, leveling, contouring
  • Mechanics of Structures — Bending stress, shear stress, deflection of beams, columns
  • Building Construction & Technology — Foundations, brick masonry, plastering, pointing, roofs
  • Civil Engineering Drawing-I (AutoCAD) — Residential plans, elevations, section drawings
  • Concrete Technology — Mix design, slump test, admixtures, curing methods

Practical Labs: Surveying Field Camp-I, Concrete Testing Lab, AutoCAD Civil Lab.

Semester 4: Advanced Surveying, Hydraulics & Transportation

Core Subjects:

  • Advanced Surveying (Surveying-II) — Theodolite, tachometry, Total Station, GPS mapping
  • Hydraulics & Fluid Mechanics — Hydrostatic pressure, orifices, notches, pipe flow, open channels
  • Transportation Engineering — Highway alignment, geometric design, bituminous pavements, traffic
  • Design of Reinforced Concrete Structures (RCC) — Working stress & limit state methods, beams, slabs
  • Soil Mechanics & Foundation Engineering — Grain size analysis, shear strength, bearing capacity

Practical Labs: Total Station Survey Camp, Hydraulics Lab, Geotechnical Lab.

Semester 5: Design of Steel Structures, Water Supply & Estimation

Core Subjects:

  • Design of Steel Structures — Tension members, compression members, roof trusses, connections
  • Water Supply & Sanitary Engineering — Water purification, sewage treatment, pipeline networks
  • Quantity Surveying, Estimation & Costing — Bill of quantities (BOQ), rate analysis, tender docs
  • Irrigation Engineering — Canals, headworks, dams, micro-irrigation, hydrology
  • Elective: Railway, Bridge & Tunnel Engineering / Green Buildings

Practical Labs: Environmental Engineering Lab, Computer-Aided Civil Estimations, Minor Project.

Semester 6: Construction Management, Safety & Capstone Project

Core Subjects:

  • Construction Management & Accounts — CPM/PERT scheduling, site logistics, contract law
  • Maintenance & Rehabilitation of Structures — Structural audits, retrofitting, waterproofing
  • Valuation of Land & Real Estate — Depreciation, rental methods, valuation reporting
  • Elective: Earthquake Resistant Design / Pre-stressed Concrete

Capstone & Internship: Comprehensive Civil Live Project, 4-Week On-Site Industry Training & Viva.

Admission & Progression Pathways

Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya provides structured progression pathways for civil engineering aspirants:

  • Regular Entry (3 Years / 6 Semesters): Passed 10th Standard (High School) with Mathematics and Science from any recognized state or central board.
  • Lateral Entry to 2nd Year (2 Years / 4 Semesters): Passed 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics/Chemistry OR 2-year ITI Certificate in Draughtsman Civil, Surveyor, or allied trades.
  • Progression to B.Tech: Direct lateral entry eligibility into the 2nd Year B.Tech Civil Engineering across top AICTE universities.
