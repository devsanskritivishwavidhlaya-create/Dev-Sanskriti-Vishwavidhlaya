Program Overview

The Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Fire & Safety Engineering at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya is designed to prepare high-caliber safety professionals and fire protection engineers equipped to prevent, evaluate, and mitigate fire and industrial hazards across high-risk sectors including chemical plants, oil & gas, manufacturing, construction, aviation, and mega-infrastructure projects.

The curriculum blends core engineering sciences with state-of-the-art safety technologies, practical fire ground drills, hydraulic simulations, hazard identification methodologies (HAZOP/HIRA), and statutory compliance under the Factories Act, National Building Code (NBC), and international safety standards (ISO 45001, NFPA, OSHA).

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Engineering Fundamentals

Core Subjects:

  • Engineering Ethics & Professional Values — Ethical standards, workplace integrity, social responsibility
  • Communication & Technical Writing — Report presentation, technical documentation, standard operating procedures
  • Engineering Mathematics - I — Differential calculus, linear algebra, vector spaces
  • Computer Programming using C — Algorithm design, structured programming, logic building
  • Basic Electrical & Electronics Engineering — Circuits, instrumentation, power distribution basics
  • Basics of Fire Engineering & Combustion — Fire chemistry, triangle and tetrahedron of fire, classes of fire
  • Workshop Practice & Engineering Graphics — Mechanical tools, fabrication, orthographic projections

Semester 2: Core Engineering & Thermal Sciences

Core Subjects:

  • Engineering Mathematics - II — Integral calculus, differential equations, Laplace transforms
  • Engineering Chemistry & Hazardous Materials — Flammables, corrosives, reactive chemicals, toxicology
  • Engineering Physics & Thermal Science — Heat transfer, thermodynamics, radiation physics
  • Fluid Mechanics & Fire Hydraulics — Flow through pipes, Bernoulli equation, nozzle discharge, water hammer
  • Strength of Materials & Structural Safety — Stress, strain, beam deflection, structural fire resistance
  • Environmental Science & Industrial Ecology — Pollution abatement, eco-regulations, sustainability
  • Fire Protection Systems & Equipment Lab — Extinguishers, hose reels, personal protective gear testing

Semester 3: Fire Science & Safety Management

Core Subjects:

  • Principles of Safety Management — Safety policy, Heinrich domino theory, safety organization and metrics
  • Fire Dynamics & Flame Propagation — Heat release rate, enclosure fires, flashover, backdraft, plume theory
  • Building Construction & Fire Safety Design — NBC classifications, compartmentation, fire rated walls, egress design
  • Chemical Process Safety & Reaction Hazards — Exothermic runaway reactions, runaway containment, MSDS analysis
  • Pumping Systems & Water Supply for Fire Fighting — Centrifugal pumps, hydrants, pressure boosting, water storage reservoirs
  • Electrical Safety & High Voltage Protection — Static electricity, hazardous area classification, arc flash, grounding
  • Fire Hydraulics & Foam Systems Lab — Pump characteristic curves, foam proportioner testing, hydrant drills

Semester 4: Industrial Safety & Hazard Analysis

Core Subjects:

  • Industrial Safety Engineering — Material handling, plant layout, crane safety, confined space entry protocols
  • Fire Prevention & Protection Systems — Automatic sprinklers, deluge systems, dry chemical and gaseous flooding
  • Hazard Identification & Risk Assessment (HIRA) — What-If analysis, FMEA, fault tree analysis, Bow-Tie method
  • Explosion Protection & Pressure Relief Systems — Deflagration, detonation, rupture discs, explosion suppression
  • Occupational Health & Industrial Hygiene — Dust, toxic vapors, noise dosimetry, biological hazards, threshold limits
  • Machine Guarding & Mechanical Safety — Interlocks, light curtains, OSHA guarding standards, lockout-tagout (LOTO)
  • Safety Instrumentation & Gas Detection Lab — Combustible gas detectors, VOC monitors, sound level meters

Semester 5: Process Industries & Advanced Suppression

Core Subjects:

  • Safety in Chemical & Petrochemical Industries — Refining units, storage terminals, BLEVE mitigation, flare systems
  • Safety in Construction & High-Rise Structures — Scaffolding, excavations, formwork, fall protection systems
  • Automatic Fire Detection & Alarm Systems — Smoke, thermal, beam, flame detectors, addressable panels, EN54 / NFPA 72
  • Quantitative Risk Analysis (QRA) & HAZOP — Consequence modeling, hazard and operability studies, individual risk contours
  • Fire Fighting Vehicles & Special Equipment — Foam tenders, turntable ladders, aerial platforms, breathing apparatus
  • Transportation & Logistics Safety — Dangerous goods transport, PESO norms, hazchem coding, emergency response guide
  • Fire Ground Operations & Simulation Lab — Structural fire simulations, SCBA confidence maze, foam application drills

Semester 6: Disaster Planning & Environmental Auditing

Core Subjects:

  • Disaster Management & Emergency Preparedness — On-site and off-site emergency plans, table-top exercises, incident command
  • Nuclear Radiation Safety & Cryogenic Safety — Ionizing radiation shielding, liquid oxygen / nitrogen storage hazards
  • Safety Audit & Environmental Management Systems (ISO 45001) — IS 14489 audit framework, management reviews, documentation
  • Smoke Control & Ventilation Design — Stairwell pressurization, atrium smoke evacuation, smoke damper controls
  • Legal Aspects of Safety & Factories Act — Factories Act 1948, Petroleum Act, SMPV Rules, Workmen's Compensation
  • Industrial Ergonomics & Human Factors Engineering — Work physiology, human error analysis, manual material handling
  • Emergency Response & Rescue Drill Lab — High-angle rope rescue, victim extrication, triage and emergency first aid

Semester 7: Advanced Modeling & Specialized Sectors

Core Subjects:

  • Advanced Fire Dynamics & Computer Modeling (FDS) — CFD smoke flow simulations, Pyrosim, Pathfinder evacuation modeling
  • Offshore Safety & Petroleum Exploration Hazards — Platform layouts, blowout preventers, subsea safety, hydrogen sulfide
  • Safety in Mines & Heavy Engineering Works — Mine fire containment, blast safety, heavy fabrication safety
  • Environmental Pollution Control & Hazardous Waste — Hazardous waste handling, effluent treatment, scrubbers, incinerators
  • Minor Project & Industry Internship Phase-I — Field study in industrial plant, hazard audit, supervisor evaluation
  • Advanced Fire Suppression Systems Lab — Clean agent discharge testing, water mist systems, inert gas systems

Semester 8: Capstone Project & Industry Residency

Core Components:

  • Major Capstone Project — Independent, industry-sponsored hazard audit, safety system design, or modeling thesis
  • Industrial Internship (12 Weeks) & Case Analysis — Full-time immersion in petrochemical refinery, manufacturing plant, or safety consultancy
  • Comprehensive Viva Voce — Oral examination covering entire 4-year engineering curriculum evaluated by academic & industry experts
