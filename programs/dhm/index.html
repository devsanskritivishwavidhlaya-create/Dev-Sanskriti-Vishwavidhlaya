Program Overview

The Diploma in Hotel Management (D.H.M.) at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya prepares aspiring professionals for global opportunities across the hospitality, tourism, travel, and culinary industries. The program combines culinary science, guest relations, accommodation operations, and hotel administrative skills with personal refinement and ethical Indian hospitality values (Atithi Devo Bhava).

With fully equipped mock kitchens, training restaurants, front office labs, and housekeeping simulation suites, students gain hands-on operational excellence followed by mandatory star-category hotel industrial training.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Hospitality Foundations & Basic Culinary Skills

Core Subjects:

  • Introduction to Culinary Arts — Kitchen hierarchy, culinary terminology, knife skills, stocks, sauces, and cooking methods
  • Food & Beverage Service - I — Service styles, cutlery, crockery, table setting, and customer handling
  • Front Office Operations - I — Organizational structure, reservation systems, check-in, and guest reception
  • Housekeeping Operations - I — Cleaning agents, guest room cleaning, linen management, and hygiene

Practical / Lab:

  • Basic Training Kitchen (BTK) practicals, restaurant layout drills, and guest handling simulations

Semester 2: Intermediate Operations & Service Science

Core Subjects:

  • Regional Indian Cuisine & Bakery Basics — Tandoor, Mughlai, South Indian cuisines, bread & pastry basics
  • Food & Beverage Service - II — Non-alcoholic & beverage service, bar operations, billing procedures
  • Front Office Accounting & PMS — Night auditing, property management systems, guest folio handling
  • Accommodation Management — Interior design, pest control, safety & security in housekeeping

Practical / Lab:

  • Quantity Training Kitchen (QTK) production, banquet setups, and PMS software training

Semester 3: Mandatory Industrial Exposure Training (20 Weeks)

Industrial Internship in Star Hotels:

  • Food Production Rotation (5 Weeks) — Continental, Indian, Garde Manger, Bakery
  • Food & Beverage Service Rotation (5 Weeks) — Fine dining restaurants, banquet service, room service
  • Front Office Rotation (5 Weeks) — Concierge, reception, bell desk, reservation desk
  • Housekeeping Rotation (5 Weeks) — Public areas, guest rooms, linen & laundry operations

Assessment:

  • Industrial training log book submission, performance appraisal by hotel GM, and comprehensive presentation/viva

Semester 4: Advanced Operations, Cost Control & Management

Core Subjects:

  • International Cuisine & Advanced Bakery — French, Italian, Chinese cookery, confectionery art
  • F&B Management & Cost Control — Portion control, menu engineering, beverage costing, inventory control
  • Hospitality Sales & Marketing — Digital hotel marketing, OTA channel management, event planning
  • Hotel Facility Planning & Maintenance — Kitchen layout design, fire safety, eco-friendly hotel practices

Practical / Lab:

  • Advance Training Kitchen (ATK) culinary showcase, banquet management project, and exit viva
