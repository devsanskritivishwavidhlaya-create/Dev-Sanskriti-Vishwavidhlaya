Program Overview

The Master of Science (M.Sc.) Chemistry program at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya provides advanced knowledge in all major branches of chemistry. The curriculum integrates theoretical concepts with extensive practical laboratory work, preparing graduates for careers in research, industry, pharmaceuticals, and academia.

Students gain hands-on experience with modern analytical instruments including NMR, HPLC, GC-MS, FTIR, UV-Vis spectrophotometers, and computational chemistry software. The program emphasizes both fundamental chemistry and interdisciplinary applications.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Foundation Chemistry

Core Subjects:

  • Inorganic Chemistry - I — Coordination chemistry, organometallics, symmetry & group theory
  • Organic Chemistry - I — Reaction mechanisms, stereochemistry, named reactions
  • Physical Chemistry - I — Thermodynamics, chemical kinetics, quantum chemistry

Practical / Lab:

  • Inorganic Chemistry Lab, Organic Chemistry Lab, Physical Chemistry Lab

Semester 2: Advanced Chemistry

Core Subjects:

  • Inorganic Chemistry - II — Bioinorganic chemistry, solid state chemistry, nuclear chemistry
  • Organic Chemistry - II — Heterocyclic chemistry, pericyclic reactions, spectroscopy
  • Physical Chemistry - II — Electrochemistry, surface chemistry, statistical thermodynamics

Practical / Lab:

  • Advanced Inorganic Lab, Advanced Organic Lab, Instrumental Methods Lab

Semester 3: Specialization & Analytical

Core Subjects:

  • Analytical Chemistry — Chromatography, spectroscopy, electroanalytical methods
  • Computational Chemistry & Cheminformatics — Molecular modeling, DFT calculations, QSAR
  • Green Chemistry & Industrial Chemistry — Catalysis, polymers, nanomaterials

Elective I (Choose One):

  • Medicinal Chemistry & Drug Design
  • Environmental Chemistry
  • Food Chemistry & Analysis
  • Materials Chemistry

Semester 4: Research & Dissertation

Core Subjects:

  • Research Methodology — Literature review, experimental design, statistical analysis
  • Spectroscopic Methods of Structure Determination — NMR, MS, IR, UV-Vis interpretation

Project / Dissertation:

  • Dissertation — Original research project under faculty guidance with viva voce
  • Internship — Industrial/research lab internship (80 hours minimum)
