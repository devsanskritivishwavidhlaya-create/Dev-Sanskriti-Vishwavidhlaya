Program Overview

The Bachelor of Acupuncture (B.Ac.) at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya is an undergraduate professional degree designed to train competent, compassionate, and scientifically grounded healthcare practitioners in the discipline of acupuncture and natural healing sciences. The curriculum bridges ancient therapeutic wisdom with contemporary biomedical understanding.

Students receive exhaustive theoretical knowledge and extensive hands-on clinical training in meridian pathways, precise acupoint localization, safe needling protocols, pulse and tongue diagnosis, moxibustion, cupping, electro-acupuncture, and clinical pain rehabilitation. Guided by experienced clinicians, graduates learn to manage acute and chronic pain, neuro-musculoskeletal disorders, stress-induced ailments, and systemic imbalances holistically.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Foundations of Biomedical Sciences & TCM

Core Subjects:

  • Human Anatomy & Histology I — Osteology, arthrology, myology, and neurovascular topography
  • Human Physiology I — Cell physiology, neuromuscular transmission, blood, cardiovascular and respiratory systems
  • Foundations of Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) — Concept of Qi, Blood, Body Fluids, Yin-Yang philosophy, and Five Element theory
  • Public Health, Hygiene & First Aid — Aseptic techniques, sterilization, needle hygiene, CPR, and infection control

Practical / Lab:

  • Surface Anatomy & Palpation Lab, Demonstration of Needles & Sterilization Procedures

Semester 2: Meridian Pathways & Acupoint Anatomy

Core Subjects:

  • Human Anatomy & Histology II — Visceral anatomy (gastrointestinal, urinary, endocrine, and reproductive systems)
  • Human Physiology II — Digestion, renal excretion, neuroendocrine control, and systemic homeostasis
  • Meridian Channels & Acupoints I — Course, internal pathways, and acupoints of Lung, Large Intestine, Stomach, Spleen, Heart, and Small Intestine channels
  • General Pathology & Microbiology — Inflammation, cellular adaptation, bacterial and viral pathogens in healthcare

Practical / Lab:

  • Precise Acupoint Localization Lab I, Anatomical Landmark Mapping, Needle Grip Simulations

Semester 3: Advanced Channels, Diagnostics & Needling Protocols

Core Subjects:

  • Meridian Channels & Acupoints II — Urinary Bladder, Kidney, Pericardium, San Jiao, Gallbladder, Liver, Ren Mai, and Du Mai channels
  • Diagnostic Methods in Holistic Medicine — The Four Examinations (Inspection, Auscultation/Olfaction, Inquiry, Palpation); Tongue & Pulse diagnosis
  • Acupuncture Needling Techniques & Manipulation — Insertion depth, angles, lifting/thrusting, twirling, De-Qi elicitation, and management of needle emergencies
  • Pathophysiology & Western Clinical Medicine — Pathogenesis of common medical and neurological conditions

Practical / Lab:

  • Acupuncture Techniques Lab, Pulse & Tongue Diagnostic Workshops, Phantom Model Needling Practice

Semester 4: Energetics, Moxibustion & Physical Modalities

Core Subjects:

  • Point Energetics & Specific Acupoints — Five Shu points, Yuan-Source, Luo-Connecting, Xi-Cleft, Back-Shu, Front-Mu, and Hui-Meeting points
  • Moxibustion, Cupping & Ancillary Therapies — Direct and indirect moxibustion, fire cupping, wet cupping, Gua Sha, and plum blossom needling
  • TCM Internal Medicine Protocols — Etiology and acupuncture treatment for digestive, respiratory, and metabolic conditions
  • Research Methodology & Biostatistics — Evidence-based alternative healthcare, clinical trials design, and statistical data interpretation

Practical / Lab:

  • Moxibustion & Cupping Practical Sessions, Electro-Acupuncture Machine Operation & Safety Lab

Semester 5: Clinical Acupuncture, Pain & Microsystems

Core Subjects:

  • Musculoskeletal & Pain Management Acupuncture — Cervical spondylosis, lumbar disc prolapse, sciatica, arthritis, frozen shoulder, and sports injuries
  • Microsystem Acupuncture Sciences — Auricular (ear) acupuncture, Yamamoto New Scalp Acupuncture (YNSA), and reflex zones
  • Specialized Therapeutics & Demographics — Women's health, obstetrics/gynecology, pediatric acupuncture, and geriatric wellness
  • Clinical Case Analysis & Ethics — Patient consent, legal medical jurisprudence, record keeping, and professional conduct

Clinical Posting I:

  • Supervised Outpatient Clinic Posting I (Patient intake, case history formulation, treatment planning)

Semester 6: Advanced Practice, Integrative Health & Internship

Core Subjects:

  • Integrative Medicine & Rehabilitation — Multidisciplinary patient care, neuro-rehabilitation, and lifestyle therapeutics
  • Clinic Management, Health Laws & Regulations — Establishing private practices, safety certifications, clinical waste disposal, and entrepreneurship
  • Advanced Clinical Therapeutics — Management of psychosomatic disorders, insomnia, chronic fatigue, and autoimmune pain syndromes

Internship & Dissertation:

  • Supervised Clinical Internship II — Full-time rotational clinical internship delivering patient acupuncture therapy under medical guidance
  • Bachelor's Dissertation / Case Project — Evidence-based clinical study report and clinical case presentation
  • Comprehensive Viva Voce — Grand oral examination covering all theoretical and clinical domains
