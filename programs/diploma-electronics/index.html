Program Overview

The Diploma in Electronics & Electrical Engineering at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya integrates the fundamentals of electrical circuits and power systems with advanced electronic device engineering, microcontrollers, and modern digital telecommunications. The curriculum aligns with the rapidly burgeoning Indian electronics manufacturing, 5G communications, and smart automation sectors.

Students receive rigorous hands-on training in PCB schematic design, soldering and SMD rework, oscilloscope signal troubleshooting, Arduino and ARM microcontroller firmware programming, and industrial sensor interfacing. Lateral entry into the 2nd year (3rd semester) is available for 10+2 (Science/Vocational) or 2-year ITI (Electronics/COPA/Electrician) candidates, alongside seamless transition to B.Tech 2nd year.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Foundation in Applied Sciences & Basic Electricals

Core Subjects:

  • Applied Mathematics-I — Matrices, algebra, trigonometry, differential calculus
  • Applied Physics-I — Wave motion, lasers, semiconductors, electrostatics
  • Applied Chemistry — Semi-conducting materials, etching reagents, dielectric polymers
  • Engineering Graphics & Drawing — Circuit schematic drafting, projections, CAD basics
  • Communication Skills in English — Technical communication, presentation & grammar

Practical Labs: Applied Physics Lab, Applied Chemistry Lab, Basic Electrical & Electronics Workshop.

Semester 2: Fundamental Electronics & Circuit Analysis

Core Subjects:

  • Applied Mathematics-II — Integral calculus, differential equations, complex variables
  • Electronic Devices & Components — PN junctions, Zener diodes, BJT, FET, MOSFET characteristics
  • Circuit Theory & Networks — Mesh and nodal analysis, network theorems, resonance
  • Electronic Instruments & Measurement — CRO, DSO, multimeters, function generators
  • Environmental Science & Green Electronics — E-waste recycling, eco-friendly design norms

Practical Labs: Semiconductor Devices Lab, Electronic Instruments Lab, Circuit Simulation (Multisim).

Semester 3: Digital Logic & Analog Circuits (Lateral Entry Point)

Core Subjects:

  • Digital Electronics — Boolean algebra, logic gates, multiplexers, flip-flops, counters, shift registers
  • Analog Electronic Circuits — Audio amplifiers, feedback amplifiers, oscillators, op-amp 741 applications
  • Electrical Machines & Power Supplies — DC motors, single-phase transformers, linear & SMPS power supplies
  • Signals & Systems Basics — Continuous & discrete signals, Fourier transform basics
  • Electronic Workshop & PCB Design — Schematic capture, PCB track layout, etching, drilling, soldering

Practical Labs: Digital Logic Lab, Analog Electronics Lab, PCB Design & Fabrication Lab.

Semester 4: Microprocessors, Communication Systems & Power Electronics

Core Subjects:

  • Microprocessor 8085 / 8086 Architecture — Assembly language programming, memory & I/O interfacing
  • Principles of Communication Engineering — AM, FM, superheterodyne receivers, noise analysis
  • Power Electronics & Drives — SCR, TRIAC, IGBT, inverters, choppers, speed control of motors
  • Linear Integrated Circuits — 555 timers, phase-locked loops (PLL), active filters, voltage regulators
  • Data Communication & Computer Networks — OSI model, TCP/IP, Ethernet, routing basics

Practical Labs: Microprocessor Lab, Communication Engineering Lab, Power Electronics Lab.

Semester 5: Microcontrollers, Embedded Systems & IoT

Core Subjects:

  • Microcontrollers (8051 & ARM Cortex) — Architecture, timers, interrupts, embedded C programming
  • Digital & Wireless Communication — PCM, ASK, FSK, PSK, cellular mobile architecture (4G/5G)
  • Internet of Things (IoT) & Sensors — Sensor interfacing, NodeMCU / ESP32, MQTT protocols, cloud dashboards
  • Optical Fiber Communication — Fiber modes, optical sources (LED/Laser), photodetectors, optical link budget
  • Elective: Biomedical Electronics / VLSI Design (VHDL/Verilog)

Practical Labs: Microcontroller & Embedded C Lab, IoT Sensor Lab, Minor Hardware Project.

Semester 6: Industrial Automation, Maintenance & Capstone Project

Core Subjects:

  • Industrial Automation (PLC & SCADA) — Ladder logic programming, HMI interfaces, industrial sensors
  • Troubleshooting & Maintenance of Electronic Equipment — Diagnostic procedures, test equipment, SMD repair
  • Entrepreneurship & Industrial Management — Startups in electronic hardware, business ethics
  • Elective: Consumer Electronics & Smart Home Tech / Satellite Communication

Capstone & Internship: Comprehensive Hardware Capstone Project, 4-Week Electronics Industry Internship & Viva.

Admission & Lateral Entry Options

Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya offers flexible admissions and career progression pathways:

  • Regular Entry (3 Years / 6 Semesters): Passed 10th Standard (High School) with Science and Mathematics from any recognized state/central board.
  • Lateral Entry to 2nd Year (2 Years / 4 Semesters): Passed 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics, Chemistry/Vocational Subjects OR 2-year ITI Certificate in Electronics Mechanic, Electrician, Instrument Mechanic, or Information Tech.
  • Progression to B.Tech: Direct lateral entry eligibility into the 2nd Year B.Tech in Electronics & Communication (ECE) or Electrical & Electronics (EEE).
