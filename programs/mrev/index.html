Program Overview

The Masters in Real Estate Valuation (MREV) at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya is designed to produce qualified real estate valuers who can meet the growing demand in the Indian real estate sector. The curriculum covers property valuation, land economics, real estate law, construction technology, and modern valuation methodologies.

Graduates gain expertise in property assessment, investment analysis, urban planning, and regulatory frameworks, making them ready for careers with government agencies, banks, real estate firms, and as independent valuers.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Foundation of Real Estate

Core Subjects:

  • Introduction to Real Estate — Indian real estate industry overview, market dynamics, RERA Act
  • Property Valuation Principles — Valuation concepts, approaches (market, income, cost)
  • Land Economics — Land use patterns, supply-demand dynamics, urban land markets

Practical / Lab:

  • Property valuation exercises, market survey project, case studies

Semester 2: Advanced Valuation Methods

Core Subjects:

  • Valuation Methods & Techniques — Discounted cash flow, capitalization, comparison method
  • Real Estate Finance — Mortgage analysis, funding structures, financial modeling
  • Real Estate Law & Legislation — Property laws, RERA, land acquisition, stamp duty

Practical / Lab:

  • Valuation reports, financial analysis projects, legal documentation exercises

Semester 3: Specialization

Core Subjects:

  • Construction Technology & Estimation — Building materials, cost estimation, project management
  • Urban Planning & Development — Master plans, zoning, infrastructure development
  • Real Estate Investment & Portfolio Management — REITs, portfolio optimization, risk management

Elective I (Choose One):

  • Environmental Impact Assessment
  • Facility Management
  • Smart Cities & Urban Development

Semester 4: Research & Practice

Core Subjects:

  • Research Methodology — Research design, data analysis, report writing
  • Professional Ethics & Practice — Valuer code of conduct, regulatory compliance, client management

Project / Dissertation:

  • Dissertation — Original research project on real estate valuation under faculty guidance
  • Internship — Real estate firm / government agency internship (80 hours minimum)
