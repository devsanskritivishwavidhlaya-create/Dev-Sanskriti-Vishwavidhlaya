A comprehensive 4-semester postgraduate program exploring cognitive, clinical, developmental, and behavioral psychology with hands-on research and practical training.

4 Semesters 2 Years Duration
Graduation B.A. in Any Stream
₹12,000 Per Semester Fee

Program Overview

The Master of Arts (M.A.) in Psychology at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya is designed to provide students with a deep understanding of human behavior, mental processes, and psychological theories. The program integrates academic rigor with DSVV's holistic education philosophy, preparing graduates for careers in clinical practice, counseling, research, and organizational psychology.

Students gain practical experience through laboratory work, field visits, internships, and supervised research projects alongside comprehensive coursework in core psychological domains.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Foundation of Psychology

Core Subjects:

  • Introduction to Psychology — History, scope, schools of thought, research methods
  • General Psychology — Perception, learning, memory, emotion, motivation
  • Physiological Psychology — Brain-behavior relationship, neurotransmitters, biological bases of behavior

Electives / Skill Papers:

  • English / Hindi Communication
  • Elementary Statistics for Psychology

Semester 2: Applied & Social Psychology

Core Subjects:

  • Social Psychology — Group dynamics, attitude formation, social cognition, prejudice, conformity
  • Developmental Psychology — Lifespan development theories (Piaget, Erikson, Vygotsky), aging, death
  • Psychology of Personality — Trait, psychoanalytic, humanistic, behavioral approaches to personality

Electives / Skill Papers:

  • Environmental Studies
  • Indian Psychology & Yoga

Semester 3: Clinical & Counseling Psychology

Core Subjects:

  • Clinical Psychology — Psychopathology, assessment, diagnosis, and therapeutic interventions (CBT, REBT, psychodynamic)
  • Counseling Psychology — Counseling theories, techniques, ethical guidelines, therapeutic relationship
  • Psychological Testing & Assessment — Intelligence tests, personality inventories, projective techniques, aptitude testing

Electives / Skill Papers:

  • Health Psychology
  • Industrial / Organizational Psychology

Semester 4: Research & Specialization

Core Subjects:

  • Research Methodology in Psychology — Quantitative & qualitative research, experimental design, statistical analysis (SPSS), research ethics
  • Neuropsychology — Brain mapping, neuropsychological assessment, cognitive rehabilitation
  • Positive Psychology & Well-being — Happiness, resilience, strengths-based approach, mindfulness

Project / Dissertation:

  • Dissertation — Original research project under faculty guidance with viva voce
  • Internship — Supervised clinical/counseling practice (80 hours minimum)
