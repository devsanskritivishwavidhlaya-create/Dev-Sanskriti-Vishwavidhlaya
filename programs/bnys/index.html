Program Overview

The Bachelor of Naturopathy & Yogic Sciences (B.N.Y.S.) at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya is a professional degree program recognized by the Central Council of Indian Medicine (CCIM). This 5.5-year integrated program trains students in the principles and practice of naturopathy, yoga therapy, and traditional Indian healing sciences.

The curriculum combines modern medical sciences with traditional naturopathic and yogic practices, preparing graduates to become licensed naturopathy doctors capable of providing holistic healthcare using natural therapies, yoga, and drugless treatment modalities.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1 & 2: Foundation Sciences

Human Anatomy, Human Physiology, Basics of Biochemistry, fundamentals of Yoga, Sanskrit language, Community Medicine, and Environmental Studies. Introduction to naturopathy principles.

Semester 3 & 4: Core Naturopathy

Pathology, Microbiology, Pharmacology, Yoga Philosophy & Practice, Naturopathic Diagnostics, Hydrotherapy, Manipulative Therapy, and Dietary Therapy principles.

Semester 5 & 6: Advanced Therapeutics

Advanced Yoga Therapy, Herbal Medicine, Chromotherapy, Acupuncture, Clinical Naturopathy, Fasting Therapy, Mud Therapy, and Physiotherapy fundamentals.

Semester 7 & 8: Clinical Practice

Clinical diagnosis & management, General Medicine, Surgery, Obstetrics & Gyneciatrics, Pediatrics, Dermatology, and Naturopathic case-taking methodology.

Semester 9 & 10: Specialization & Research

Advanced clinical practice, Research methodology, Medical ethics, Yoga for specific diseases, Naturopathy in chronic diseases, and supervised clinical rotations.

Internship (1 Year): Supervised Clinical Training

Mandatory 1-year rotating internship in naturopathy hospitals and yoga therapy centers with supervised patient care and clinical practice.
