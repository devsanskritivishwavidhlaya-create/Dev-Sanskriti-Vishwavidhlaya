Program Overview

The Diploma in Veterinary Pharmacy (D.V.P.) at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya is an industry-aligned professional diploma designed to meet the growing national demand for competent paramedical and pharmacological professionals in veterinary healthcare, livestock husbandry, and pharmaceutical sectors.

Students receive rigorous hands-on clinical and laboratory instruction in animal anatomy, physiological mechanisms, veterinary pharmacology, dosage compounding, immunization schedules, toxicology, and hospital dispensary administration. The program also emphasizes value-based animal welfare, compassionate care, and rural livestock empowerment in alignment with DSVV's holistic educational ethos.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Foundations of Veterinary Anatomy, Physiology & Pharmacy

Core Subjects:

  • Veterinary Anatomy & Physiology (DVP-101) — Comparative osteology, arthrology, myology, and vital organ systems in domestic ruminants, equines, canines, and poultry.
  • General Principles of Veterinary Pharmacy & Pharmacognosy (DVP-102) — Sources of drugs, weights and measures, pharmaceutical terminology, Latin abbreviations, and indigenous medicinal plants used in veterinary care.
  • Animal Husbandry & Livestock Management (DVP-103) — Breeds of cattle, buffalo, sheep, goat, and swine; housing design, farm sanitation, breeding systems, and clean milk production.
  • Biochemistry & Nutrition of Domestic Animals (DVP-104) — Essential nutrients, digestion and metabolism in ruminants vs. non-ruminants, feed formulation, and nutritional deficiency prevention.

Practical / Lab:

  • Practical: Veterinary Anatomy & Dispensing Lab I (DVP-105) — Skeletal identification, organ anatomy demonstration, weighing and measuring, and preparation of simple pharmaceutical mixtures.

Semester 2: Veterinary Pharmacology, Microbiology & Formulations

Core Subjects:

  • Veterinary Pharmacology & Chemotherapy (DVP-201) — Pharmacokinetics, autonomic drugs, antimicrobial agents, anthelmintics, antiprotozoals, NSAIDs, and analgesics for veterinary clinical use.
  • Veterinary Microbiology & Immunology (DVP-202) — Morphology, staining, culture methods for animal pathogens; antigens, antibodies, active and passive immunity, and biological product preservation.
  • Pharmaceutical Formulations & Dosage Forms (DVP-203) — Compounding of liquid orals, ointments, liniments, boluses, drenches, electuaries, and parenteral injections.
  • Animal First Aid & Emergency Nursing Care (DVP-204) — Management of hemorrhage, fractures, bloat, choke, dystocia assistance, wound dressing, bandaging, and vital signs monitoring.

Practical / Lab:

  • Practical: Veterinary Pharmacology Lab II (DVP-205) — Antiseptic preparation, dosage calculations, sterilization protocols, antibiotic sensitivity screening, and mock prescription dispensing.

Semester 3: Toxicology, Disease Control & Dispensary Regulations

Core Subjects:

  • Veterinary Toxicology & Poisoning Management (DVP-301) — Plant poisoning (hydrocyanic acid, nitrate/nitrite, oxalate), pesticide toxicity, snake bites, antidote administration, and toxicological sampling.
  • Livestock Diseases & Immunization Protocols (DVP-302) — Major infectious diseases (FMD, Black Quarter, Anthrax, Hemorrhagic Septicemia, Brucellosis, Rabies); vaccine schedules and cold chain maintenance.
  • Veterinary Jurisprudence, Ethics & Drug Regulations (DVP-303) — Drugs & Cosmetics Act, Pharmacy Act, Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act, Schedule H & X prescription norms, and veterinary professional code of conduct.
  • Clinical Pathology & Diagnostic Techniques (DVP-304) — Routine hematology, urinalysis, fecal sedimentation for helminth eggs, skin scraping for mange mites, and blood smear examination for hemoprotozoa.

Practical / Lab:

  • Practical: Clinical Pathology & Drug Compounding Lab III (DVP-305) — Blood smear staining, fecal floatation techniques, compounding specialized veterinary lotions, ointments, and suspension preparations.

Semester 4: Hospital Dispensary Management, Special Animals & Clinical Internship

Core Subjects:

  • Hospital & Veterinary Dispensary Management (DVP-401) — Inventory management, FIFO systems, cold storage monitoring, biomedical waste segregation, prescription auditing, and computerized billing.
  • Animal Reproduction & Breeding Pharmaceuticals (DVP-402) — Reproductive physiology, estrus synchronization agents, hormonal therapies, artificial insemination equipment handling, and obstetrical pharmacy.
  • Avian, Exotic & Wildlife Healthcare Pharmaceuticals (DVP-403) — Poultry medication protocols, pet bird therapeutics, canine/feline specialty care, and emergency pharmaceuticals for wildlife sanctuaries.

Clinical Internship & Field Training:

  • Clinical Veterinary Hospital Internship & Field Training (DVP-404) — Mandatory hands-on rotation in veterinary clinical complexes, government polyclinics, dairy cooperatives, and livestock farms under veterinary surgeons.
  • Project Work, Case Study & Comprehensive Viva Voce (DVP-405) — Submission of clinical record file, case history documentation, presentation of specialized dispensing study, and oral examination.
