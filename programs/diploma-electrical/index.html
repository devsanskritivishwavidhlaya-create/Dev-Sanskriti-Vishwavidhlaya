Program Overview

The Diploma in Electrical Engineering at Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya prepares students for high-impact careers in power utilities, manufacturing plants, green energy installations, electrical grid maintenance, and automation systems.

Through state-of-the-art electrical machinery laboratories, simulation software (MATLAB, Multisim), and industrial field visits, students develop in-depth expertise in AC/DC machine winding, electrical sub-station maintenance, power electronic converters, and PLC-based industrial automation. Lateral entry is open for 10+2 (Science/Vocational) or 2-year ITI (Electrician/Wireman) certificate holders directly into the 3rd semester.

Semester-wise Curriculum

Semester 1: Foundation in Applied Sciences & Electrical Essentials

Core Subjects:

  • Applied Mathematics-I — Determinants, matrices, trigonometric expansions, differential calculus
  • Applied Physics-I — Electrostatics, current electricity, magnetic fields, semiconductors
  • Applied Chemistry — Battery chemistry, insulating oils, dielectric materials
  • Engineering Graphics & Drawing — Orthographic projections, electrical circuit symbology
  • Communication Skills in English — Verbal communication, technical comprehension, reporting

Practical Labs: Applied Physics Lab, Applied Chemistry Lab, Electrical Wiring & Fitting Workshop.

Semester 2: Electrical Engineering Fundamentals & Components

Core Subjects:

  • Applied Mathematics-II — Integral calculus, differential equations, Fourier series basics
  • Basic Electrical Engineering — Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, AC circuits, 3-phase systems
  • Basic Electronics — Diodes, BJTs, rectifiers, filter circuits, operational amplifiers
  • Electrical Workshop & Safety Measures — Domestic wiring, earth pit installation, safety codes
  • Environmental Studies & Energy Conservation — Energy audits, green energy basics

Practical Labs: Basic Electrical Lab, Electronics Hardware Lab, Electrical Jointing Practice.

Semester 3: Network Theory & DC Electrical Machines (Lateral Entry Point)

Core Subjects:

  • Electrical Circuit Theory — Thevenin, Norton, Superposition, Maximum Power Transfer theorems
  • Electrical Machines-I — DC generators, DC motors, speed control, efficiency tests
  • Electrical Measurements & Measuring Instruments — PMMC, MI meters, wattmeters, energy meters, bridges
  • Electronics Devices & Analog Circuits — FET, MOSFET, transistor amplifiers, oscillators
  • Electrical CAD & Drafting — Single line diagrams, panel layout drafting using CAD

Practical Labs: DC Machines Lab, Electrical Measurements Lab, Electronics Circuits Lab.

Semester 4: AC Machines & Power Generation Systems

Core Subjects:

  • Electrical Machines-II — 1-phase & 3-phase transformers, induction motors, alternators
  • Generation of Electrical Power — Thermal, hydroelectric, nuclear and solar power stations
  • Transmission & Distribution of Electrical Power — Over-head lines, corona effect, underground cables
  • Power Electronics — SCR, TRIAC, DIAC, inverters, choppers, cycloconverters
  • Digital Electronics & Microprocessor 8085 — Logic gates, flip-flops, microprocessor architecture

Practical Labs: AC Machines & Transformer Lab, Power Electronics Lab, Microprocessor Lab.

Semester 5: Switchgear, Protection & Industrial Automation

Core Subjects:

  • Switchgear & Protection — Circuit breakers (SF6, Vacuum, Air Blast), protective relays, isolators
  • Utilization of Electrical Energy & Traction — Electric heating, welding, illumination, electric traction
  • Industrial Automation & PLC — Programmable Logic Controllers, SCADA basics, ladder logic
  • Electrical Estimation, Costing & Contracting — Residential & industrial electrification estimates
  • Elective: Renewable Energy Sources (Solar PV & Wind Systems)

Practical Labs: Switchgear & Relay Lab, PLC Automation Lab, Minor Project.

Semester 6: Electric Drives, Sub-station Practice & Capstone Project

Core Subjects:

  • Electric Drives & Control — Solid-state motor drives, VFDs (Variable Frequency Drives)
  • Sub-station Engineering & Maintenance — Sub-station layouts, transformer maintenance, earthing
  • Industrial Management & Smart Grid Tech — Smart meters, tariff structures, energy efficiency
  • Elective: Electric Vehicles (EV) Technology & Battery Management

Capstone & Internship: Comprehensive Electrical Live Project, 4-Week Grid/Sub-station Training & Viva.

Admission & Lateral Entry Options

Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya offers flexible enrollment and progression opportunities for electrical diploma students:

  • Regular Entry (3 Years / 6 Semesters): 10th Standard (High School) passed with Science and Mathematics from any recognized state/central board.
  • Lateral Entry to 2nd Year (2 Years / 4 Semesters): Passed 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics/Chemistry, OR 2-year ITI Certificate in Electrician, Wireman, or Electrical Maintenance.
  • Progression to B.Tech: Direct lateral entry eligibility into the 2nd Year B.Tech Electrical Engineering at DSVV and top engineering colleges.
